The Cradle of Life Marketing Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Plain-language summary: The Marketing Studio is an owner-operated production tool. It connects only to accounts the operator authorizes, stores integration credentials on the operator's local computer, and publishes media only after explicit approval.

Scope

This policy applies to The Cradle of Life Marketing Studio and its connections to social platforms, including TikTok. The Marketing Studio is not offered as a public consumer service and does not accept public user registrations.

Information used

When an account is connected through a platform's authorization flow, the Marketing Studio may access the minimum account information and permissions required for the enabled feature. Depending on the platform, this may include:

  • Basic account identifiers and display information.
  • Authorization tokens supplied by the platform.
  • Media metadata and publishing status.
  • Approved videos, images, titles, captions, and related post settings selected for publication.

The Marketing Studio does not request or store a social-platform password.

How information is used

Account information is used only to identify the connected account, prepare approved media, upload or publish approved posts, and display available publishing results. It is not sold, rented, used for unrelated advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Storage and retention

Integration credentials and production records are stored locally on the operator's computer and are excluded from the public game repository. Platform providers process information under their own privacy policies. Local credentials and related account records are retained until the integration is disconnected or the local data is removed.

Publishing and disclosure

Media and accompanying post text are transmitted to a connected platform only when the operator authorizes that publishing action. Once published, the post is governed by the destination platform's settings, terms, and privacy practices.

Security

The Marketing Studio is bound to the local computer, uses platform authorization flows where available, and does not intentionally expose its management interface to the public internet. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Choices and deletion

Access can be revoked through the connected platform's account settings. The operator can also disconnect the account and remove locally stored credentials and production records. Published posts can be managed through the destination platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated when integrations or data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be directed through the official contact methods listed in the The Cradle of Life press kit.
