About the game

One-sentence description

29 words

The Cradle of Life is a dark-fantasy tactical RPG about Caelis, a warrior searching for the Needle after a Shimmer takes his wife and whose decisions attract six gods.

Short description87 wordsOpen

The Cradle of Life is a dark-fantasy tactical RPG about Caelis, a warrior searching for the Needle after a Shimmer takes his wife. Combat takes place on a grid where movement and attacks use Action Points. Positioning, height, reach, wounds, Poise, Hunger, and Psych affect each encounter. The opening takes place in Thornvale, where players question people and search for quests and clues. Six gods respond to Caelis's decisions, and each has a different plan for the world. The Steam demo covers the opening section in Thornvale.

Long description158 wordsOpen

Shimmers are appearing without warning. They alter familiar places, and sometimes people or entire villages disappear. After a Shimmer takes his wife, Caelis searches for the Needle, a forgotten relic that may explain what happened. The search begins in Thornvale. Objectives leave the route open. Players progress by questioning people, reading records, and checking roads, homes, caves, ruins, and forests. Combat uses a tactical grid and Action Points. Movement and attacks draw from the same pool. Height, weapon reach, armor, wounds, Poise, fear, and terrain affect each turn. Hunger and Psych continue outside combat, so Caelis's condition when a fight begins matters. Players can customize Caelis's appearance across his world sprite and portrait. Weapon familiarity and Resolve develop through play. Six gods respond to Caelis's decisions, and each one has a different plan for the world. The Steam demo contains the opening section in Thornvale. It introduces the Shimmers and the gods while leaving their larger role unresolved.

Systems in the current demo

How it plays

The systems below are playable now in the Steam demo.

Tactical combat

Movement and attacks use the same Action Point pool. Positioning, height, reach, terrain, wounds, armor, Poise, and fear determine the available choices.

Exploration

Players find quests and clues by talking to people, reading records, and checking the environment. Objectives leave the route open.

The Shimmers

Shimmers alter familiar places, enemies, and terrain. The demo shows their effects and leaves their origin unresolved.

Hunger and Psych

Hunger and Psych continue outside combat. Low values carry into encounters and affect Caelis from the first turn.

Character customization

Players choose Caelis's appearance, hair, beard, colors, and equipment. His world sprite and portrait reflect the same choices.

The six gods

The gods respond to Caelis's decisions. Their reactions depend on what he says and does, and each god has a different plan for the world.

About Garrett

Garrett Mickelsen

Garrett Mickelsen is the solo developer of The Cradle of Life, an independent tactical RPG made in RPG Maker MZ. He is also an active-duty U.S. Marine officer and military historian. The game began as a personal project built around his interests in history, mythology, and storytelling. Some of its oldest lore came from stories his father told around campfires when he was young.

Most of the work is done in his spare time around military service and family life. The game combines exploration, player choice, character customization, and grid-based combat in a dark fantasy setting of gods and forgotten places.

The Cradle of Life is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Marine Corps or Department of Defense.

For press and creators

Creator & Press Guidelines

Coverage is welcome. These guidelines apply to the public demo and approved materials supplied through this press kit.

Creator Monetization Policy
Yes. Content creators are welcome to monetize videos, livestreams, reviews, previews, guides, and other original content featuring The Cradle of Life through advertising, subscriptions, donations, sponsorships, or similar platform-supported monetization. No separate permission is required.
Streaming & Video Policy
You are welcome to stream, record, review, discuss, and create videos featuring The Cradle of Life, including the public demo. Gameplay footage, screenshots, trailers, and materials supplied through this press kit may be used for editorial and creator coverage of the game. No prior approval is required, and criticism—positive or negative—is welcome.
Spoiler Guidance
Creators and press may show and discuss anything contained in the publicly available demo. We ask that major late-demo story revelations, god-related choices, and the final sequence not be used prominently in thumbnails, titles, or social-media previews without a spoiler warning. Full playthroughs and spoiler discussions are completely welcome when appropriately labeled.
Typical Demo Playtime
Approximately 1 hour. Players who explore optional quests, dialogue, and lore may take longer.
Content warnings
Dark-fantasy violence, psychological horror themes, disturbing imagery, references to abuse and implied sexual violence, and mature story content. No explicit nudity or explicit sexual content.
Music & Footage Usage
Official screenshots, trailers, GIFs, and gameplay footage provided in this press kit may be used for editorial, review, preview, and creator coverage of The Cradle of Life. In-game music and audio may be included as part of captured gameplay or official footage. Please do not extract, redistribute, or upload individual music tracks or game assets as standalone content.
